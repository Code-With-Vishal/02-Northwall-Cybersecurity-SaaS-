Northwall / threat detection and response

See the attack before it lands.

  1. Northwall watches laptops, servers, email, sign-ins and cloud apps from one console.
  2. It joins related alerts into one incident, so your team reads one story instead of a pile of alerts.
  3. When an attack is confirmed, it isolates the device, revokes the session and blocks the address.
  4. Every incident ends with a written report your leadership and insurer can read.
  5. Built for companies of 50 to 500 people with a small IT team and no night shift.
